How to research responsibly

  1. Start with an original document, official statement or dataset when possible.
  2. Compare multiple credible sources and distinguish reporting from opinion.
  3. Check the author, publication date, corrections and the context of quoted claims.
  4. For elections, laws or public health, check the relevant authority in the correct country or jurisdiction.

These are external sources, not OPERAVA publications or endorsements. This page is an educational resource directory, not a live news feed, election guide or claim of political affiliation.
