Understanding currency conversion

Multiply the amount by the quoted exchange rate for your selected currency pair. Verify the rate timestamp before financial decisions. Published reference rates can differ from the rate a bank or card issuer actually offers.

Frequently asked questions

Are these exchange rates live?

No. The app fetches the provider’s latest available rates and shows their update time. They are not continuously streaming trade prices.

Are fees included?

No. Conversion results do not include provider spreads, transfer fees or taxes.
